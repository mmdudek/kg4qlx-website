 Tuning From Scratch
  1. Tuner TX=5, ANT=0, IND=L
  2. TX carrier @ 5w. Adjust IND, then TX for lowest reflected power.
  3. Increase ANT & adjust TX for lowest reflected power.
  4. Repeat step 3 until lowest reflected power achieved.
  5. Reduce IND one position (L=lowest). Adjust the TX and ANT for lowest SWR. Continue until lowest SWR cannot be repeated.
  6. Increase IND one position (A=highest). Tune for lowest SWR.
